DSH Mail

DSH Mail is a personal tool that lets my own DeepSeek Harness desktop assistant read, sort, label and (only after I approve each message) send email from my own mail accounts. It is not offered to anyone else.

Privacy policy

Who uses it. Only the owner of this page, on their own Windows computer.

What it accesses. With my permission through Google sign-in, it uses the Gmail scope https://mail.google.com/ to read, search, label, move and delete mail and to send mail from my account.

Where data goes. Email is fetched straight from Google to my computer over IMAP and SMTP. Nothing is sent to any server run by this app, because there is none. The sign-in token is stored on my computer, encrypted with Windows DPAPI. Email content may be sent to the AI model I choose for my assistant, only when I ask it to work with that email.

Sharing. No data is sold, shared or used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access. Delete the connection at myaccount.google.com/connections, and the local account with setup.js remove.
